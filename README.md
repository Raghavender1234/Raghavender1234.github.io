## 🎯 Career Pathways & Featured Engineering Case Studies

Recruiters and hiring managers can explore my work through three targeted entry points:

### 1. 🚀 Simulation Engineering (CFD / FEA / MATLAB)
* **NACA 0015 Airfoil CFD Optimization (ANSYS ICEM CFD & Fluent)**: C-shaped domain (-20c to +40c), multi-block structured meshing with first-layer wall spacing $y=3.3\times 10^{-6}\text{ m}$ ($y^+ < 1$), and a 4-stage grid independence study (21,274 to 104,274 nodes, $<0.5\%$ delta between Fine and Super Fine, peak $L/D=48.36$).
* **Offshore Wind Monopile Buckling (Abaqus FEA)**: 40 m hollow steel monopile ($R=3.75\text{ m}, t=0.08\text{ m}$), 23,200 structured quad shell elements, 3D datum depth and quadrant partitioning, 5-mode linear perturbation eigenvalue buckling under combined eccentric nacelle weight and hydrodynamic pressure.
* **Wind Turbine Rotor BEM & Offshore Wave Kinematics (MATLAB & XFOIL)**: Radial induction factor convergence algorithm, NACA 0030 polars, blade pitch/twist optimization, and Airy wave particle trajectory simulation (74% module mark).

### 2. 🔬 Materials, Corrosion & Laboratory R&D
* **High-Temperature Molten Salt Corrosion of WAAM SS316L (MSc Thesis)**: Wire Arc Additively Manufactured SS316L tested in ternary $10\text{NaNO}_3\text{-}78\text{KNO}_3\text{-}12\text{KCl}$ molten salt at 600°C for 250 h and 500 h in a horizontal tube furnace under nitrogen purge (open vs. covered-lid crucibles); 30-position stitched 3D optical profilometry (`.zvi`), $10^{-5}\text{ g}$ precision gravimetry, and SEM-EDS cross-sectional elemental mapping.
* **Lead-Free Semiconductor Solar Paint (Industrial Group Project with Nissan)**: Formulated bismuth methyl iodide quantum dot dispersion (1.8–2.3 eV bandgap) in a $\text{TiO}_2$ matrix on galvanized automotive steel; 48-hour electrical conductivity and stability testing.
* **Ship Hull Marine Corrosion Hypothesis Testing (Assessing Risk & Failure)**: Controlled single, double, and triple epoxy coating thickness comparison under simulated seawater attack.

### 3. 📐 Mechanical Design & Project Engineering
* **Smart Shower Cold-Water Recirculation Prototype (Component Design — 73%)**: SolidWorks 3D CAD modeling, Bambu Studio PLA 3D printing, solenoid valves, and sensor logic targeting a 50–70% reduction in pre-bath cold water waste.
* **Stafford Cripps SC3 Lecture Theatre Modernization (Project Lead)**: Double Diamond framework, Project Charter, circular furniture reuse, UK/ISO standards compliance (BS EN 12464-1, ISO 3382-3, BS 8300), and 15-item quantitative PM Risk Assessment Matrix (reducing PI scores from 25 to $\le 9$).
